## 1. Overview & Privacy Commitment
**Imanvo** ("Faith, Every Day." / "ہر دن، ایمان کے ساتھ") is built with a strict **Privacy-First, Zero-Tracking** architecture. We believe personal worship, Holy Quran reading habits, and spiritual supplications should remain strictly confidential to the individual user.

* **No Accounts Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to use any feature of Imanvo.
* **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** The app contains zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking pixels.
* **No Data Selling:** We never sell, lease, or monetize your information under any circumstances.

---

## 2. Permissions & Data Usage

### A. Location Information (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)
* **Purpose:** Device location is accessed solely to calculate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and to compute the exact compass direction (Qibla Azimuth) pointing toward the Holy Kaaba in Makkah.
* **On-Device Computation:** Solar calculations are performed locally on your device. Your coordinates are never sent to private external tracking servers or stored remotely.
* **User Control:** Users can deny GPS access and select their city manually from the built-in offline global database at any time.

### B. Device Sensors (Magnetometer & Accelerometer)
* **Purpose:** Accessed in real-time solely to orient the interactive Qibla compass needle. Sensor data is ephemeral and is never logged or recorded.

### C. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
* **Purpose:** Delivers optional Daily Dua notifications at the user's preferred time.
* **Local Scheduling:** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Android's native system AlarmManager without remote push tracking servers.

### D. Device Storage (Jetpack DataStore)
* **Purpose:** Holy Quran bookmarks, last-read Surah and Ayah positions, custom Dhikr counters, and theme preferences are saved locally in the device's secure internal sandbox.

## 3. Third-Party Network Services
The application operates 100% offline. If an active internet connection is present, the app may connect to the verified AlAdhan API to synchronize live prayer timetables for your coordinates. No personal identity, advertising ID, or device telemetry is transmitted.
